Scope: billing/Stripe, tenant isolation, notification routing. Raw output, not yet verified by the orchestrator — see findings.md for verification status.

1. **Plan changes create additional paid subscriptions — P1.** Evidence: [billing.py:266](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/routers/billing.py:266), [service.py:364](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/service.py:364), [service.py:431](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/service.py:431). Upgrade/downgrade always creates Checkout with `mode="subscription"`; it never updates the existing Stripe subscription. Different plans use different idempotency keys. A paying Starter tenant upgrading to Pro can therefore retain both recurring charges while the local row tracks only one. **Fix:** modify the existing subscription or route established subscribers through the billing portal; serialize initial checkout creation per tenant.

2. **Disabled tenant administrators retain billing authority — P1.** Evidence: [billing.py:88](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/routers/billing.py:88), [tenant_admin.py:249](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/routers/tenant_admin.py:249). Disabling an administrator invalidates their tenant session but leaves their platform membership intact. The billing resolver checks role and membership but ignores `User.is_active`. Their independently authenticated platform identity can still cancel the subscription, obtain portal access, and read invoices through the owner session. **Fix:** require an active target user on every billing authorization.

3. **Successful resubscription can remain permanently canceled locally — P1.** Evidence: [webhooks.py:206](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:206), [webhooks.py:249](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:249). If `checkout.session.completed` arrives first for a canceled tenant, it replaces `stripe_subscription_id` without clearing `canceled`. Subsequent active updates for that new ID hit the stale-update guard and are ignored. The customer pays but remains locked out. **Fix:** distinguish cancellation of a particular Stripe subscription from tenant subscription state; reconcile the new subscription’s status when accepting checkout completion.

4. **Late payment failures defeat cancellation enforcement — P1.** Evidence: [webhooks.py:380](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:380), [periodic.py:309](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/tasks/periodic.py:309). After deletion marks a subscription canceled, a delayed `invoice.payment_failed` unconditionally changes it to `past_due`. Delivered before the enforcement job, this removes the tenant from the cancellation cutoff indefinitely. **Fix:** validate the invoice’s subscription identity and reconcile current subscription state; never revive a terminal subscription from an invoice event.

5. **Events for old subscriptions overwrite replacement subscriptions — P1.** Evidence: [webhooks.py:261](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:261), [webhooks.py:325](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:325). Handlers resolve by tenant/customer without ensuring the event concerns the current subscription. A delayed deletion of `sub_old` cancels an active `sub_new`; an old update can replace its ID, plan, and period. **Fix:** track subscription generations, reject events for superseded IDs, and serialize reconciliation. Same-subscription plan updates also need protection against stale delivery.

6. **Stripe updates undo administrative suspension — P1.** Evidence: [platform_admin.py:304](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/routers/platform_admin.py:304), [service.py:235](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/service.py:235), [webhooks.py:298](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:298). Administrative deactivation schedules period-end cancellation and disables the tenant. The resulting subscription update can still carry `active`, causing the webhook to reactivate it immediately. **Fix:** record suspension reason separately; payment recovery may clear billing suspension, never an operator suspension.

7. **Repeated cancellation extends unpaid access indefinitely — P1.** Evidence: [service.py:197](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/service.py:197), [billing.py:347](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/routers/billing.py:347). For a trial without a Stripe subscription, cancellation moves an elapsed `current_period_end` to now—even when already canceled. Posting cancellation every day keeps the three-day grace window ahead of enforcement. **Fix:** make cancellation idempotent and preserve the original cancellation/access-expiry timestamp.

8. **Nonpaying states other than canceled have no access cutoff — P1.** Evidence: [webhooks.py:263](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:263), [periodic.py:247](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/tasks/periodic.py:247), [periodic.py:309](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/tasks/periodic.py:309). Incoming statuses such as `unpaid` or `incomplete_expired` are stored verbatim. Trial expiry excludes Stripe-linked subscriptions, and access enforcement selects only `canceled`. Such tenants retain access without further events. **Fix:** define explicit entitlement and grace rules for every supported subscription state and reconcile them periodically.

9. **Reactivation bypasses paid seat/contact limits — P2.** Evidence: [tenant_admin.py:277](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/routers/tenant_admin.py:277), [customers.py:432](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/routers/customers.py:432), [usage.py:70](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/usage.py:70). Usage counts only active accounts, while reactivation performs no limit check. An administrator can disable accounts, invite replacements within quota, then reactivate everyone. **Fix:** apply the quota gate to every inactive-to-active transition.

10. **Concurrent creation exceeds plan limits — P2.** Evidence: [usage.py:143](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/usage.py:143), [order_service.py:367](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/services/order_service.py:367), [order_service.py:310](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/services/order_service.py:310). Usage checking and insertion are not serialized. Concurrent requests can each observe the final available slot and all succeed. Even order creation acquires its tenant lock only after checking quota. **Fix:** acquire a common tenant/quota lock before counting and retain it through creation; use the same mechanism for invitations and uploads.

11. **Notification fallback bypasses authorization and consent — P2.** Evidence: [notification_service.py:238](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/services/notification_service.py:238), [notification_service.py:253](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/services/notification_service.py:253). When no active staff remain, the resolver sends directly to `tenant.billing_email`, without checking account access or notification preferences. If that address belongs to a disabled, opted-out former owner, a customer comment still sends them order information and its excerpt. **Fix:** require an explicitly authorized, consenting fallback recipient; billing-address presence alone must not authorize order notifications.

12. **Out-of-order refunds are permanently lost — P2.** Evidence: [webhooks.py:434](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/webhooks.py:434), [billing.py:769](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/routers/billing.py:769), [service.py:503](/Users/vaclav/workspace/assoluto/app/platform/billing/service.py:503). A refund arriving before its local invoice returns successfully, committing the event deduplication record without retaining the refund. Later `invoice.paid` records it as paid. That handler also overwrites existing refund status. **Fix:** persist unresolved refunds or retry reconciliation, and prevent older payment events from erasing refund state.
